HUD & Screen-Space Redesign

The world viewport and tile art are untouched — this is a chrome pass. Today's HUD is a full-width text block stacked below the view, roughly as tall as the map itself. The redesign moves everything into compact overlays on top of the viewport, uses icons and bars instead of monospace dumps, and pushes the recipe list behind a toggle.

Crafting & controls
"Another one stops moving. The Verge keeps what it is owed."
Viewport reclaims the full frame — vitals, noise, minimap, resources and controls are compact overlays that only occupy their corners; the recipe dump moves behind a Tab panel; narration is a transient toast, not a permanent line.
Vitals → 4 full-width text bars become a compact icon+bar cluster, always visible, ~15% of the space.
Noise → gets its own top-center gauge since it's the core mechanic, not buried between hydration and health.
Inventory → a collapsed chip tray shows carried goods; a caret expands the full pack grid on demand instead of two dense text lines always on.
Controls → the 8-line recipe list moves to a Tab-toggled panel; the always-on bar shows only 1-2 verbs actually usable right now.
Narration → the Grey King's lines become a fading toast instead of a permanent HUD line competing with everything else.
Net gain → viewport keeps its full 512px height; HUD chrome drops from ~240px of dedicated space to overlays inside the existing frame.

Further improvement notes
Crafting as a menu, not raw number keys — right now 0-9/B/L/P/O/V each fire a specific recipe from memory. Keep the keys as shortcuts, but let Tab open a real menu (mockup above) you can also click/select — new players shouldn't need the README open in another tab.
A hotbar for what you're carrying — spear/sword/knife/axe currently only show as text in the kit line. A small slot row (like the resource tray) showing your equipped/held tools would read faster than parsing a sentence.
"T offer / G give" needs a visible state — what you're currently offering to trade is buried in the controls text. Surface it as a small highlighted chip near the resource tray so a nearby player (and you) can see it without reading a sentence.
Skill levels are invisible progress right now — "wood 0 hunt 0 butc 0..." reads as a debug line. Small tick-marks or a level pip per skill (shown only in the expanded pack panel, not the always-on HUD) would make leveling feel like progress instead of a stat dump.
Noise threshold needs a clearer "you're about to be seen" signal — today it's a bar changing color. A screen-edge vignette pulse or a crow-count readout the moment you cross CROW_THRESHOLD would teach the core mechanic faster than the number alone.
Standing/marked state deserves an icon, not a bracketed clause — "[marked, standing -40]" is easy to miss mid-sentence. A small badge next to the status chip (neutral/marked/notorious) reads at a glance.
Narration toasts should queue, not overwrite — the Grey King's lines and death notices currently share one 2-line log. Two toasts stacking briefly (with the older one fading first) avoids losing a line to a fast second event.
Minimap could mark your fire(s) — terrain-only is right for hiding creatures, but a soul's own built fires are already public knowledge (global, not fogged) — a small dot for "your fire" would help with a map nine times the original size.
Color-blind safety — health/warmth/hydration all lean on red vs. green vs. blue at a glance. Since bars now carry an icon shape too (circle/diamond/square), that shape is already a non-color cue — worth keeping deliberately as the redesign is implemented, not dropped for polish.
